## Proposal identity

Proposal: V4-010. Status: proposed, not accepted. Acceptance evidence: none. Repository number: unassigned. Owner: Naim. Base repository must be reconciled at J4-M00.

## Context

Passive capture, sleep mode and cross-app aggregation expand privacy and authority risk.

## Decision

Use purpose-scoped context, typed observations, retention controls and owner-absence defaults that narrow external authority.

## Alternatives considered

Recording everything indefinitely creates exposure and false inferences. Presence heartbeats must not silently authorize work.

## Security and privacy impact

No background recording of others, hidden messages or high-impact sleep-time lease actions.

## Consequences

This adds explicit review and test work at the affected boundary. It reduces ambiguous authority but does not eliminate operator error, malicious administrators, provider defects, or test blind spots. The owning design is [NIGHT_MODE.md](../../../../docs/NIGHT_MODE.md).

## Migration and compatibility

Migrate sources individually with consent, provenance, deletion and offline tests. Preserve current implementations until the relevant mission proves compatibility. A staged rollback must restore the previous accepted implementation without reviving expired authority.

## Acceptance evidence

Pending. Require exact base/head, test report, owner authorization and trusted gate result. Do not fill these with a fabricated success.
