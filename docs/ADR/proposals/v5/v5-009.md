## Context

## Decision

Keep per-system source records, evidence quality and candidate dispositions. Adopt a mechanism only through a separately approved decision.

## Alternatives considered

A best-of-all-tools installation creates overlapping memory ownership and redundant orchestrators. Ignoring research loses useful design evidence.

## Security and privacy impact

Do not import unvetted skills, credentials or creator claims as authority.

## Consequences

This adds explicit review and test work at the affected boundary. It reduces ambiguous authority but does not eliminate operator error, malicious administrators, provider defects, or test blind spots. The owning design is [2026-09-external-build-lessons.md](../../../research/2026-09-external-build-lessons.md).

## Migration and compatibility
