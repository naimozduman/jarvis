Use [the per-system record](2026-09-external-build-lessons.md) and governance/RESEARCH_SOURCES.json. Do not maintain a second architecture in research prose.

For a new finding, record the exact source, date, version/commit when meaningful, evidence class, reproducible experiment and candidate JARVIS boundary. An accepted adoption points to its ADR and tests. An impressive demo or an AI-written VERIFIED label does not satisfy that gate.

Conflicting reports remain conflicting until primary evidence resolves them. Claims not rechecked during this pack's creation stay attributed to their source.
