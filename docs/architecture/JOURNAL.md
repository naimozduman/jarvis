# Purpose

Journal stores the owner's subjective reflection.

JARVIS may ask, organize, retrieve, and summarize. It must not fabricate feelings or turn inferred mood into owner-authored journal content.

## Entry types

- free text,
- voice transcript plus optional audio reference,
- guided reflection,
- linked event/day/project/relationship entry.

## Provenance

Generated summaries are labeled generated. Owner-authored text remains distinguishable from model interpretation.

## Prompts

Prompts may use the Life Ledger to ask useful questions. They should be low-friction and configurable.

## Privacy

Journal entries may be restricted or local-only. Relationship/intimate entries may live in a dedicated vault with summarized Core references.

## Memory

Journal statements do not automatically become permanent memory. The memory engine may create candidates with source references.
