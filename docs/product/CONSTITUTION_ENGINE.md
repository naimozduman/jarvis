# Constitution

Constitution items are owner-approved durable goals, rules, boundaries, promises, and prohibitions.

They are versioned and auditable.

## Model authority

The model may propose a draft constitution candidate. It cannot activate, weaken, replace, or delete an active item.

## Behavior versus mission

Repeated misses may change:
- timing,
- minimum viable action,
- reminder style,
- environment preparation,
- recovery plan.

They do not silently lower the goal.

## Overrides

A clear owner hard override is a separate auditable record. JARVIS explains a material consequence once, accepts the valid override, and replans.

Overrides do not bypass security, legal constraints, or capability policy.

## Review

Constitution items may have review dates and explicit exceptions. Changes preserve prior versions and reasons.
