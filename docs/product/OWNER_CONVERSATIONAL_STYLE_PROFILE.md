# Future owner conversational style profile

Reconciled October 5, 2026. This document retains compatible detailed requirements from the R1 reconstruction. [Current decisions](../product/DECISIONS.md) and V5 subsystem owners control newer phasing and authority. Source citations remain historical evidence, not current provider/device verification.

`owner_conversational_style_profile` is a deliberately inactive future input. It is not read by
the Brain, prompt assembler, router, memory pipeline, policy engine, or any transport.

If the owner later explicitly approves creation of a profile, its source material may include only
owner-approved examples of English casual conversation, Turkish casual conversation, natural
Turkish-English code-switching, and close-friend or relationship conversational rhythm. The
profile must describe bounded presentation preferences, not facts, commitments, identity claims,
or authority.

It must remain separate from canonical memory, must have provenance and owner approval, and must
not be populated from an external conversation corpus. It is not training data and does not permit
model fine-tuning. A later activation would require a separate review of privacy, retention,
prompt provenance, evaluation, and cross-surface behavior.
